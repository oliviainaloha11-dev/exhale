Need to talk to someone?

If things feel like too much right now, you don't have to carry it by yourself. Reaching out is a brave thing to do. These are free, confidential, and there for you any time, day or night.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

    Call or text 988

    Talk with a trained, caring person. You don't need to be in crisis to reach out. Feeling overwhelmed counts. Free, 24/7, in the US.

    Call 988 Text 988 Chat online

  • Crisis Text Line

    Text HOME to 741741

    If talking out loud feels hard, texting works too. A trained volunteer will text back. Free, 24/7, in the US.

    Text HOME to 741741

  • A trusted adult

    Sometimes the best first step is someone who already knows you: a parent or guardian, a school counselor, a teacher you like, a coach, an older relative, a doctor. You can even show them this page if the words are hard to find.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

Outside the US?

findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines in many countries.

A gentle note

exhale is a quiet place to take a breath, but it isn't a replacement for real support from real people. Needing more help than a breathing exercise isn't a failure. It just means you're human. You deserve to feel okay.
