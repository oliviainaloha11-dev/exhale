About exhale

A free, quiet corner of the internet for students who need a minute.

Why this exists

School can be a lot: tests, deadlines, everything in between. exhale is a place to put it down for a few minutes. Breathe, stretch, write something out, or just listen to the rain. Then go back to your work a little lighter.

It was made by a high school student, for other students.

I’m a high school student in California, and it’s pretty competitive here. I’m tired a lot. I wanted to take care of myself, but even wellness takes energy, especially when an app is complicated or gives you a lot of things to do. So I made exhale really simple. You don’t have to take a separate break for it. You can use it while you’re studying, or in between. Wellness shouldn’t be something you have to set aside time for. It should be part of your day, all the time.

— Olivia

Private, for real

  • No ads, no accounts, no cookies.
  • Everything happens inside your own browser. Nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.
  • The mind jar and unsent letter don't save anything at all. When they're gone, they're gone.
  • Two things are remembered on your device only: your journal draft (so you don't lose it if the tab closes) and your study timer settings. You can clear the draft any time with "Clear my draft."
  • We count visits anonymously (just totals, like "40 visits this week") so we know if exhale is helping. It never knows who you are. Read the full privacy page.

Free, always

No premium version, no paywall, no sign-up. It's a static website, so it costs almost nothing to keep online.

Not medical advice

These are simple calming tools, not therapy or treatment. If you're struggling, please reach out to someone. You deserve real support.
